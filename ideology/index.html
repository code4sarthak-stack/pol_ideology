Political ideology guide

All 16 political ideologies, explained simply

Every person sees the world through a set of political lenses, whether they realize it or not. Should governments own essential utilities or leave them to competitive markets? Do traditions protect our communities, or do they hold people back? Does national sovereignty matter more than international cooperation? Across modern history, thinkers and everyday citizens have answered these questions in fundamentally different ways, creating 16 distinct political ideologies. Each worldview offers its own vision of fairness, prosperity and human freedom, along with real-world successes and cautionary failures. Below, you will find simple, plain-language guides to all 16 ideologies featured in the ideoMap quiz, with concrete examples from India and around the globe.

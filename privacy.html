Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Political Ideology Quiz ("we", "this website") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Your name: the name you type before the quiz is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it can be shown on your result. It is never sent to our servers or to advertisers.
  • Your answers: your answers are stored as a short code in the page address (for example ?a=0123401234) so that the quiz works page by page and you can share your result. The code does not contain your name or any contact details.
  • Language preference: saved in your browser so the site remembers whether you chose English or Hindi.

We do not ask for accounts, phone numbers, email addresses or passwords.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised ads or cookies are used, and can change their choice at any time.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type. It does not receive your name. You can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your choices

  • You can clear the saved name and language at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work as expected.

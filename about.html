Political Ideology Quiz

About this quiz

The Political Ideology Quiz is a free, 2-minute quiz available in English and Hindi. It helps you reflect on your own political views and compare them with friends and family.

How it works

You see 10 statements — five about the economy and five about society. For each one you choose how strongly you agree or disagree. Your answers are scored on two independent axes:

  • Economic (Left ↔ Right): how much the government should manage the economy, provide welfare and redistribute wealth, versus leaving it to free markets and private enterprise.
  • Social (Freedom ↔ Order): how much weight to give individual choice and civil liberties, versus tradition, shared values and a strong state.

Each answer adds between −2 and +2 points to one axis. Your final position on the two axes decides which of five results you get: Progressive Liberal, Communitarian, Libertarian, Conservative or Centrist.

Our commitment to neutrality

This quiz is not affiliated with, funded by or endorsing any political party, candidate, government body or organisation. Statements are written to present both sides of each debate fairly, and every result is described respectfully. It is a simplified model meant for fun and self-reflection — real political views are far richer than two numbers.

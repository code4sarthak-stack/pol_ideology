📖 WHAT IS IT IN SIMPLE TERMS?
-------------------------------------------------------
Maximum personal and economic freedom, with minimal government control. Libertarians believe you own your own body, time, and money. As long as you are not physically harming others or stealing, the government has no right to tell you how to live or take your hard-earned income.

🏛️ CORE PILLARS
-------------------------------------------------------
• 🗽 Maximum liberty: Complete freedom in personal choices, lifestyle, and speech.
• 💸 Drastically low taxes: Slashing government bureaucracy, spending, and regulations.
• 🤝 Voluntary agreements: Society runs entirely on free-market transactions and mutual consent.

⚖️ CULTURE & CIVIL LIBERTIES
-------------------------------------------------------
• Society & Culture: Socially permissive & live-and-let-live (-40)
• Civil Liberties:   Maximum civil freedom & minimal state (-85)

💰 ECONOMY & WORLD POLITICS
-------------------------------------------------------
• Economy:        Unregulated laissez-faire capitalism (+80)
• World Politics: Non-interventionist open trade (-25)

📖 WHAT IS IT IN SIMPLE TERMS?
-------------------------------------------------------
The popular Nordic model (like Sweden, Norway, and Denmark). It encourages private businesses and entrepreneurs to build wealth, but uses taxes and strong laws to provide free healthcare, world-class schools, paid leave, and strong pensions so that no one falls into extreme poverty.

🏛️ CORE PILLARS
-------------------------------------------------------
• 🏪 Pro-business: Private enterprise and market competition create wealth and innovation.
• 🛡️ Ironclad safety net: Universal free healthcare, free schooling, and generous pensions.
• 🤝 Strong worker rights: Fair labor laws and unions protect employees from exploitation.

⚖️ CULTURE & CIVIL LIBERTIES
-------------------------------------------------------
• Society & Culture: Inclusive & socially progressive (-40)
• Civil Liberties:   Constitutional rule of law (-30)

💰 ECONOMY & WORLD POLITICS
-------------------------------------------------------
• Economy:        Regulated markets + high safety net (-45)
• World Politics: Pro-trade with international labor standards (-35)

📖 WHAT IS IT IN SIMPLE TERMS?
-------------------------------------------------------
A system where a strong, strict, and disciplined government teams up with private businesses to drive fast-track economic modernization. It offers rapid infrastructure growth, order, and business profits, but enforces tight limits on strikes, protests, and political opposition to avoid delays.

🏛️ CORE PILLARS
-------------------------------------------------------
• 🏗️ Fast-track economic growth: Huge infrastructure projects, rapid industrialization, and investor incentives.
• 👮 Strict public discipline: Low tolerance for protests, political disorder, or labor disruption.
• 💼 State-guided markets: Leaders set national economic goals while private companies build them.

📖 WHAT IS IT IN SIMPLE TERMS?
-------------------------------------------------------
A forward-looking philosophy focused on expanding human rights, modernizing social norms, and protecting the climate. Believes that society should move beyond outdated prejudices, protect minority rights, and regulate big corporations so they do not abuse workers or pollute the planet.

🏛️ CORE PILLARS
-------------------------------------------------------
• 🌈 Equal rights: Social justice, equal opportunity, and inclusion for every community.
• 🌿 Green future: Tackling climate change through clean energy and environmental protection.
• 🛡️ Regulated capitalism: Keeping markets competitive while protecting consumers and workers.

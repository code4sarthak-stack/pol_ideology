📖 WHAT IS IT IN SIMPLE TERMS?
-------------------------------------------------------
A mix of pro-business economics and cultural stability. It strongly supports free enterprise, lower taxes, and private wealth creation, while placing deep value on family traditions, constitutional order, and the rule of law to keep society stable and peaceful.

🏛️ CORE PILLARS
-------------------------------------------------------
• 💼 Pro-market economy: Free markets, lower taxes, private investment, and rewarded hard work.
• 🏛️ Cultural stability: Valuing family roots, civic duty, and tested institutions.
• 🛡️ Cautious change: Improving society through tested, gradual reforms rather than abrupt upheavals.

⚖️ CULTURE & CIVIL LIBERTIES
-------------------------------------------------------
• Society & Culture: Respect for heritage & family values (+30)
• Civil Liberties:   Orderly rule of law & institutional checks (+10)

💰 ECONOMY & WORLD POLITICS

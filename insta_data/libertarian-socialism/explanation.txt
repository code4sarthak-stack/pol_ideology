📖 WHAT IS IT IN SIMPLE TERMS?
-------------------------------------------------------
A vision of equality without corporate bosses or authoritarian politicians. Workers run their own workplaces collectively as cooperatives, and local communities manage their own affairs through direct democratic meetings. It rejects corporate monopolies while fiercely guarding personal freedoms from government control.

🏛️ CORE PILLARS
-------------------------------------------------------
• ✊ Worker-run cooperatives: Employees make workplace decisions together and share profits.
• 🕊️ No top-down rulers: Power stays with local community meetings, not distant politicians.
• 🤝 Mutual aid: People help each other voluntarily rather than through government force.

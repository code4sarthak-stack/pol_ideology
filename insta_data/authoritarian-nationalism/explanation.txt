AUTHORITARIAN NATIONALISM
========================
Tagline: Total State Supremacy · One Nation, One Will
Key Concept: The supreme survival, honor, and unity of the nation matter far more than any individual rights.

WHAT IT STANDS FOR:
- Supreme State Authority: The government commands total authority to mobilize industry, laws, and citizens exclusively for national greatness.
- Uncompromising Loyalty: Individual dissent, strikes, and protests are banned; all citizens are expected to march in lockstep with the state.
- Formidable Armed Might: Maximum military readiness, sealed borders, and overwhelming deterrence against both foreign and internal enemies.

THE UPSIDE:
- Powerful collective unity, massive civic mobilization, and near-zero tolerance for street crime or social breakdown.
- Rapid industrialization, domestic supply-chain security, and lightning-fast governance without legislative deadlocks.

THE PRICE:
- Extreme social conformity; minority traditions and independent thinkers are suppressed as threats to uniformity.
- Loss of civil liberties, risk of total surveillance, and high danger of costly arms races or regional conflict.

REAL-WORLD CASE STUDIES:
- Historical Precedent: Otto von Bismarck's German Empire (1862–1890) — Unified principalities through state power and created the world's first worker pensions and healthcare to ensure social stability.
- Cautionary Disaster: 1930s European Fascism — Unchecked totalitarian power, racial scapegoating, and aggressive militarism led to mass atrocities and total national ruin.

About this quiz

Politics is about more than parties. This quiz looks at two big questions: how much the government should be involved in the economy, and how much it should shape the way people live. Your answers place you on a simple two-axis "political compass".

There are no right or wrong answers, and the quiz is not linked to any party or candidate. It is a quick way to reflect on your own views — and compare them with your friends.
